HISTORICAL ANALYSIS

Use the C.A.S.E. Method

C.A.S.E. is a simple way to look closely at a historical source before using it as evidence.

C - Core Identification

Identify the basic information: who, what, when, and where.

A - Action & Observation

Describe what is happening and notice important details.

S - Significance

Explain why the source matters and what it helps you understand.

E - Examine the Source

Consider the author, audience, perspective, and possible bias.
