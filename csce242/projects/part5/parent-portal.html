About Mr. Shank

Mr. Shank teaches 7th Grade Social Studies at Humphreys Middle School. The course follows U.S. history from pre-Columbian America through the Civil War.

Students work with maps, primary and secondary sources, readings, research projects, presentations, and class discussions. Assignments are posted in Google Classroom, and grades are posted in Aspen.
