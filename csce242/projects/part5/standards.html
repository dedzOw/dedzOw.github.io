DoDEA Social Studies Standards

This course aligns with DoDEA Social Studies standards for Grade 7. These are the main areas students use throughout the year.

1

History of the United States

Students study major ideas, events, developments, and turning points in United States history.

2

History of the World

Students use world-history context and multiple perspectives to better understand historical events.

3

Geography

Students examine how people, places, environments, and resources affect historical development.

4

Economics

Students explore how economic choices and systems influence people and societies.

5

Civics, Citizenship & Government

Students learn about constitutional democracy, rights, responsibilities, and government.

7.1

Native Americans

Students examine early settlements, cultural regions, and the diversity of Native American societies.

7.2

Colonial Developments

Students study European exploration, colonization, and the development of colonial America.

7.3

American Independence

Students investigate the tensions that led to the American Revolution and a new nation.
